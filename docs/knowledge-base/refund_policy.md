# UrbanCart Refund Policy

## 1. Refund Processing Timelines
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method after warehouse inspection approves returned items.
- Standard credit card and debit card refunds process within **3 to 5 business days**.
- Store credit/UrbanCart Cash refunds are processed instantly upon approval.

## 2. Partial Refunds vs Full Refunds
- **Full Refunds**: Issued for damaged items, lost shipments, defective products, or incorrect items delivered.
- **Partial Refunds**: Applied if returned items show signs of customer usage, missing original packaging, or if returned after the standard window under special management authorization.

## 3. Shipping Fee Refund Rules
- Initial shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to UrbanCart error (e.g. wrong item sent, defective product, severe delay beyond guaranteed window).

## 4. Refund Approval Hierarchy
- Automated refund recommendations up to $200 can be pre-approved by OpsPilot Agent.
- Refunds exceeding $200 or involving disputed claims require secondary human operations manager review.
